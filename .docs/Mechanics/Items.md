Consumables are the only carried items in the tower — single-use, tracked as counts on the character sheet.

## What exists

| Item | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Health potion** | Restores HP; in battle it costs 1 move, outside battle it's free |
| **Gold key** | Opens sealed doors and chests in scenes |
| **Lockpick** | Bypasses locked doors and chests in scenes |

## Rules

- **Counts, not an inventory** — the sheet tracks `consumables` as plain numbers; there is no carried-item inventory (see [[Mechanics/Equipment]])
- **Run-scoped** — consumables vanish when the run ends, like characters
- **Acquisition** — from encounter rewards and scene finds; never at start
- **Usage** — spent via `use_item` ([[Mechanics/Action Tools]])
- **In battle**, using a consumable spends 1 move from the party's round budget ([[Mechanics/Resolution]])

> Keys and lockpicks pair with the `interact` intent — doors and chests are opened by describing what you do.
