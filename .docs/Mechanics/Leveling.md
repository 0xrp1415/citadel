How characters grow during a run.

## XP

- Every encounter grants **XP to all players equally** — no player earns more than another
- Each player tracks their **own XP and level** individually; equal grants keep the party leveling together
- XP is awarded per-encounter: **8 XP × threat multiplier** per enemy, split among alive players
- **Threshold** — reaching the next level costs `level³` XP (your current level, cubed): 1 → 8 → 27 → 64 → …

## Leveling up

- Hitting a level threshold grants:
  - **+3 stat points**, banked until spent
  - **+1 max cap for every stat** — the cap is 40 at creation ([[Mechanics/Characters]]) and grows by 1 per stat per level
- Banked points are spent at **grace rooms** ([[Mechanics/Stasis]]); unspent points carry over to the next grace

## Boss reward

- Defeating a dungeon's final boss grants each player **+2 stat points**
- Players **alive** (not downed) when the boss dies get **+1 bonus** stat point

## Rules

- Stats can't exceed the current level cap — points still bank at cap, so don't bank over it
- Leveling is **run-scoped** — it resets when the run ends, like characters
