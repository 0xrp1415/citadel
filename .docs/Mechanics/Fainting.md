What happens when a character hits 0 HP.

## Downed

- At **0 HP** a player is **downed** for the rest of the encounter
- A downed player can't act that turn

## Revive

- An ally can take a **revive action** to bring them back
- Revives restore HP but leave the character vulnerable until the next turn

## Party effects

- Missing a downed player's actions **shifts targets** to the rest of the party
- A wipe (all downed) ends the run — it's the **only way** a run ends ([[Architecture/Game Flow]])
