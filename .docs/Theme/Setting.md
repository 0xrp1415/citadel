The ruined tablet stirs in your hands, a faint gold light glowing between its cracks, and the officer's voice arrives through it — flat and official, the voice of a man who has opened this gate a thousand times. **"Record begins."**

Your name is read back to you, your crime acknowledged in three spare words, your sentence pronounced: *descent into the Citadel*. The officer files your identity and issues your **call-sign**, then records the terms you will hear until the day you die — and *you will die here*; the tower has no exit, it grows and shifts to punish you, and it has never let anyone out. The tablet, he reminds you, no longer sends. You can hear; you can never answer; every word you speak is only for the tower.

Beyond the stone, the nobles of Jura are watching from a safer land, filling their ledgers with wagers on how deep you fall and how well you perform for them. He forms your **expedition** — party size, a region of the tower, a **cipher code** to share with the condemned you'll descend beside — and walks you through processing at the **staging grounds**: choose your **starter kit**, spend your *fifty points* wisely, take what you're given.

Gold, he explains, is prize money, paid out for performing well; the **merchant** sells what the tower refuses you; the rooms of **stasis** are sanctioned rest, and the tower grants them grudgingly. He notes the **scrolls** that fall from the dead, provisions of a kind.

He tells you, almost gently, that you are not his friend and not his enemy, only his *file for the evening* — and that everything you do will be judged, recorded, and reported. When everything is filed and every prisoner is ready, he pauses, lets the tablet's light hang a moment, as if deciding something, and opens the gate.

**"Go deep,"** he says, as the first hall of the tower breathes open before you. The tablet's glow fades. **"It will be recorded."**

## The flow

1. **The Gate** — inscribe your name; the officer acknowledges you
2. **Chamber Nexus** — forge a new expedition or attune to an existing cipher
3. **Staging Grounds** — pick your starter kit, allocate stats, gear up; the host opens the gate when the cadre is ready
4. **The Descent** — descend together; read the scene, submit actions, face the results
5. **The Archive** — the run ends when the party falls; the officer files the record; all players accept to return
