## Brand & Style

This design system encapsulates the atmosphere of an arcane occult academy, combining the rigorous discipline of late-Victorian scholarship with the perilous mysteries of forbidden celestial and alchemical rites. The interface functions not as an arcade game HUD, but as a living grimoire, illuminated parchment apparatus, and scholarly compendium.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Editorial Antiquity** with **Astral Mysticism**:
- **Atmosphere:** Somber, hallowed, intellectually dense, yet immaculately organized. It evokes the feeling of examining forbidden codices under guttering beeswax candlelight in a vaulted subterranean library.
- **Audience:** Players seeking immersive narrative depth, strategic dungeon crawls, arcane theorycrafting, and dark aesthetic prestige.
- **Visual Tenets:** Razor-sharp hairline brass linework, deep abyssal velvet voids, subtle celestial orbs and constellation matrices, disciplined editorial margins, and restrained incandescent violet glows that evoke etheric resonance without lapsing into high-tech neon.

## Colors

The chromatic structure establishes a resolute dark-field environment where values emerge from impenetrable shadows to warm bone highlights.

- **Foundational Neutrals:** Surfaces are composed of `abyss-void` (`#0c0b0f`) for stage depths, stepping up to `grimoire-surface` (`#181520`) and `grimoire-raised` (`#221c2e`) for codex pages, inventory slates, and modal apparatuses.
- **Primary Metal (Antique Burnished Gold):** `#d4af37` serves as the mark of scholar-prestige, celestial coordinates, active focal outlines, and sacred glyphs. Supported by `#e6ca65` for active states and `#8a702b` for aged hairline divider rules.
- **Secondary Aether (Astral Violet):** `#a855f7` and `#c084fc` denote active mana, astrological alignments, spellbook runes, and mystical anomalies. Deep ether reserves rest in `#3b0764`.
- **Tertiary Blood (Forbidden Arts & Hazard):** `#991b1b` and `#dc2626` govern vital humors, blood sacrifices, blasphemous texts, and mortal peril.
- **Text & Calligraphy (Warm Bone & Parchment):** Contrast is maintained via `#ece4d4` for primary reading and `#cfc4b0` for body notations, preventing the sterile harshness of pure white while preserving crisp readability.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances classical, high-contrast display serif forms with deeply readable bookish type reminiscent of ancient letterpress and illuminated incunabula.

- **Display & Chapter Titles:** Set in `Playfair Display`, providing dramatic thick-thin stroke transition, scholarly gravitas, and ceremonial authority.
- **Scholarly Body & Treatise Prose:** Rendered in `EB Garamond`. The font retains the humanist warmth, organic quirks, and rhythmic cadence of Renaissance printing presses.
- **Labels, Seals & Alchemical Metadata:** Rendered in uppercase roman forms using `EB Garamond` with extreme letter-tracking (`0.15em` to `0.25em`) to simulate inscriptions carved into cold stone crypts or stamped into vellum with brass dies.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adopts a **Strict Editorial Framework** rooted in historical folio proportions and classical margin rules (such as Van de Graaf canons).

- **Structure:** 12-column grid system for wide desktop viewports (`breakpoint: 1280px+`), scaling down to 8 columns on tablet (`768px - 1279px`), and 4 columns on mobile (`< 768px`).
- **Breathing Space:** Arcane interfaces frequently suffer from claustrophobic clutter; this design system demands deliberate whitespace (or void space) around major grimoire panels, letting celestial diagrams and text blocks command authority.
- **Rhythm:** All margins, paddings, and component heights adhere to a strict 4px base coordinate scale (0.25rem increments), ensuring alignment across complex multi-column spell tables and multi-tier dungeon inventories.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than conventional drop shadows or flat neon planes, depth is conveyed through **Obsidian Tonal Stratification and Burnished Hairline Framing**:

1. **Abyssal Base (Ground):** `#0c0b0f` with an optional faint, noise-stippled parchment texture at 2% opacity.
2. **Grimoire Slabs (Level 1):** `#181520` background framed by a delicate `1px` border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)` (patinated brass). No heavy drop shadow; depth relies on tonal step and border containment.
3. **Hover & Elevated Codex Slates (Level 2):** `#221c2e` surface accompanied by a subtle dual-layer shadow:
   - Ambient occlusion: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`
   - Arcane aura: `0 0 15px 0 rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.08)`
4. **Modal Reliquaries & Arcane Overlays (Level 3):** Frosted abyssal backing (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `#0c0b0f` at 85% opacity), encased in dual concentric borders: an outer `1px` border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)` and an inner `1px` border inset by `3px` of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
5. **Incandescent Resonances:** Interactive elements flare with soft optical glows (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)` for celestial gold; `0 0 12px rgba(192, 132, 252, 0.4)` for astral purple).

## Shapes

The geometry is uncompromisingly **Sharp (`roundedness: 0`)**, evoking cut obsidian, cold chiseled slate, bookbinder boards, and precision-cut astrological brass instruments.

- **Corners:** Absolutely square (`border-radius: 0px`). Where soft elements must exist (e.g., circular seals, celestial astrolabes, runic compasses), they are constructed as pure, un-squashed geometric circles (`rounded-full` / `50%`).
- **Chamfer Accents:** Select dialog headers and grimoire cards utilize 45-degree corner notches (chamfers) measuring 6px to 8px, evoking medieval book corner clasps and occult talismanic tablets.
- **Linework:** Borders are razor-thin (1px hairline), frequently adorned with tiny diamond terminal glyphs (`◇`) at corners and intersections.

## Components

### Buttons & Seals
- **Primary (Alchemical Rite):** Sharp rectangle, background in `#d4af37`, text in `#0c0b0f`, label font in tracked uppercase serif (`label-md`). Hover transitions to `#e6ca65` with an astral perimeter glow (`0 0 14px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Grimoire Inscription):** Transparent void background, `1px` border in `#8a702b`, text in `#ece4d4`. Hover fills with `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)` and shifts border to `#d4af37`.
- **Forbidden / Hazard (Blood Pact):** Sharp rectangle, background in `#581010`, border in `#991b1b`, text in `#ece4d4`. Hover flares into `#dc2626` aura.
- **Runic Seal (Circular Icon Action):** `50%` round disc, bordered by concentric brass hairlines, displaying an esoteric glyph. Rotates 45 degrees smoothly upon hover.

### Chips & Spell Badges
- Sharp-edged pill alternatives: rectangular micro-tags with `1px` borders in `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.3)` on `#181520` background. Text set in `label-sm` using `#c084fc`.
- Element affinity variants utilize color-coded hairline frames: Gold (Celestial), Purple (Astral/Void), Ruby (Blood/Forbidden), and Ochre (Transmutation).

### Input Fields & Search Incantations
- Flat dark slabs (`#121016`) bottom-bordered with a `1px` line in `#8a702b` (or fully framed with hairline brass).
- Text in `body-md` `#ece4d4`. Placeholder in `#9d9280` italicized serif.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#d4af37` accompanied by a localized violet baseline glow (`0 1px 8px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.5)`).

### Cards & Grimoire Folios
- Background in `#181520` enclosed within a hairline frame of `#8a702b`.
- Header adorned with delicate astrological dividers: thin horizontal rules terminating in miniature hollow diamonds (`◇─── § ───◇`).
- Rich editorial hierarchy: `headline-md` titles, `label-sm` category annotations in burnished gold, and generous padding (`space-xl`).

### Checkboxes, Toggles & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** Sharp square box (`16px × 16px`), `1px` border in `#8a702b`. Checked state displays a solid inner square fill of `#d4af37` or an esoteric cross seal (`+` / `×`).
- **Radios:** Concentric sharp diamond or concentric circles. Selected state illuminates a central `#c084fc` core.

### Lists & Scholar Catalogs
- Item rows separated by hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`).
- Hovering a row triggers an ambient lateral slide indicator (a gold dagger glyph `†` or astral pip) and shifts background to `#221c2e`.

### Specialized Arcane Components
- **Astrological Progress Ring:** Dual-ring circular gauge tracking mana, ritual progress, or dungeon depth, etched with degree notches and zodiacal indicators.
- **Codex Tooltips:** Framed parchment popovers with chamfered corners, showing lore excerpts, mana consumption costs, and alchemical formulas.
